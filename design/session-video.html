@devuser_99 From Session 12:40 12h ago
Why hash the token instead of just relying on JWT expiry? Isn't the JWT already self-contained?
⚡ Injected into session at 12:40
Claude replied in session
JWTs are stateless by design — no revocation without rotating the secret. Storing the hash gives us a soft-revoke flag per token, so a compromised CLI session can be killed without affecting other users or changing the signing key.
