# Download PocketAI

PocketAI is LiveShortly's app for iPhone and Mac. It runs private AI models on your own devices, and with `live daemon` on your Mac it lets you start agents and approve their tool runs from your phone.

## Get the apps

- **Mac:** [Download PocketAI for Mac](https://github.com/liveshortly/pocketai-dist/releases/latest/download/PocketAI.dmg), a notarized `.dmg` for macOS 14 and later on Apple silicon. The link always serves the newest build.
- **iPhone:** [Join the public beta on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/hEKpAQ9D). The App Store version follows once Apple approves 1.0.

Both are free. The Mac app also installs and updates the `live` CLI for you, so there is nothing else to set up. Prefer the terminal? See [Install LiveShortly](/install).

## How it fits with LiveShortly

- Sign in to LiveShortly from the Mac app to share a session or watch it from anywhere.
- Pair your iPhone with your Mac to approve agent runs from your pocket.
- Models run on your devices. Nothing you type into a local chat is sent to LiveShortly or anyone else.

More at [pocket.liveshortly.com](https://pocket.liveshortly.com), and the [PocketAI docs](/docs/pocketai).
