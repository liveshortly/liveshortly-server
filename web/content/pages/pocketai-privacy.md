# PocketAI privacy

PocketAI runs AI models on your own device. This page says what the PocketAI apps for iPhone and Mac do with your data. The hosted LiveShortly service has its own [privacy page](/privacy).

> **Owner to confirm:** the legal entity and address behind PocketAI, and the contact email below.

## The short version

- Your chats, documents and models stay on your device. PocketAI has no account and no cloud copy of your conversations.
- We don't run analytics or advertising, we don't track you, and we don't sell data.
- The apps connect to the internet only for things you ask for. They are listed below, and you can see every outbound connection in the app's network activity view.
- App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected**.

## What stays on your device

- Your conversations, projects, attached documents and photos.
- The models you download, and the text and images they generate.
- Your settings, and the licence key on a Mac.
- Voice input is processed on the device by Apple's speech recognition.

## When PocketAI uses the network

| What | When | What is sent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model downloads | You tap Download | A request to Hugging Face for the model file. No account or chat content is sent. |
| Model search | You search Hugging Face from the Models screen | Your search words. |
| Web search in chat | You switch on Tools for a chat, and the model uses it | The search words the model chooses, and the addresses of pages it reads, sent to DuckDuckGo and the sites themselves. Your other messages are not sent. |
| Pairing iPhone and Mac | You pair them | Traffic between your two devices on your own network, encrypted and pinned to the Mac's certificate. Nothing goes through our servers. |
| LiveShortly | You connect it in Settings | What the LiveShortly service stores is described on its [privacy page](/privacy). |
| Free test licence (Mac) | You ask for one | The email address you type, sent to our licence server. |
| Buying a licence (Mac) | You choose Buy | The checkout is run by our payment provider, Dodo Payments, which is the seller of record and handles payment details. We receive your email and an order number. We never see card numbers. |
| Update checks (Mac, direct download) | Automatically | A request for the update feed. |
| Refund list (Mac) | About once a day | A request for the list of refunded licence ids. It contains nothing about you. |

You can turn on Offline mode to block every outbound connection.

## Crash reports

PocketAI sends none unless you opt in, and then only technical crash data, never your content.

## Children

PocketAI is not directed at children under 13. The model catalogue is curated, but AI models can produce inaccurate or unsuitable text, so supervise young users.

## Your choices

- Delete a chat, a project or a model at any time in the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device.
- To remove an email address from our licence records, write to the contact address.
