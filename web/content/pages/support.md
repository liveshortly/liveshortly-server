# PocketAI support

Help with PocketAI on iPhone and Mac, and with your licence.

## Contact us

Email [support@pocket.liveshortly.com](mailto:support@pocket.liveshortly.com). Include your device, the app version (Settings › About) and what happened. We usually reply within two business days.

> **Owner to confirm:** that this mailbox exists and who reads it.

## Common questions

**Which models can I run?** Open the Models tab. PocketAI shows what fits your device, and warns before you download something that won't.

**Does it work offline?** Yes. After you download a model, chat works without a connection. Web search and model downloads are the only features that need one.

**How do I pair my iPhone with my Mac?** On the Mac, open Devices and choose Pair a device. On the iPhone, open the Mac tab and scan the code, or choose Pair manually and enter the address and code. Confirm that the 6-digit number matches on both screens.

**Where do my chats go?** Nowhere. They stay on your device. See the [privacy page](/pocketai-privacy).

## Licences (Mac)

- **Get a licence:** the Mac app's Settings › License has a free test licence while PocketAI is in testing.
- **Paste the key:** Settings › License, then paste it. It works offline.
- **Lost your key?** Email us from the address you used to get it and we'll resend it.
- **Refunds:** within 14 days of purchase, reply to the receipt email or write to support.

## Report a problem with a response

AI models can be wrong. Use the report action on a reply in the app, or email us the details. Never rely on a model for medical, legal or financial decisions.
